Patrol Radar – Privacy Policy

Updated November 19, 2026. Applies to the Patrol Radar Android app (io.github.missionaryj.patrolradar) and the web app at missionaryj.github.io/patrol-radar.

Patrol Radar is a public-awareness tool. It listens passively for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) advertisements that nearby equipment already broadcasts in public and shows likely public-safety equipment on a coarse map. There are no ads or analytics. Optional City Chat and Play subscription verification use a pseudonymous Supabase account ID; payment details are handled by Google Play.

Bluetooth

  • Scanning is passive only. The app never connects to, pairs with, or writes to any device.
  • Device identifiers are replaced with a salted hash. The salt is random and changes every session, so hashes cannot be linked across sessions. MAC addresses and serial numbers are not stored; serial-like text in device names is masked.
  • Phones, headphones and other consumer devices are hidden from the map by default.

Location

  • In Patrol Mode, your precise location is used to draw your position on screen and stays on your device, except when you explicitly enable optional network-dependent features described below. Choosing Navigation Mode and requesting a route sends the starting coordinates and destination coordinates to Mapbox to calculate directions, traffic estimates, and navigation. Active native navigation may also exchange location information with Mapbox for routing and map matching. Route requests are user-initiated; exact GPS coordinates are not added to Patrol Radar's crowd map.
  • Detections are snapped to a coarse grid of about 150–250 m before they are stored or shared.
  • On Android, a foreground service with a visible notification keeps scanning while the screen is off. It runs only after you tap Start and stops when you tap Stop or after 6 hours. The app does not request background (“all the time”) location.

Data stored on your device

  • Recent detections (coarse cell, category, time) are kept in app storage and expire after 15 minutes.
  • If you use the session recorder, matched police-gear records (coarse cell, hashed ID, category, signal strength, time) are kept on your device, capped at 5,000 entries, until you clear them. Exporting a session file happens only when you choose to, and you control where it goes.
  • App data is excluded from Android cloud backup.

Shared crowd map (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sharing, each report sent to patrol-radar-api.vercel.app contains only: the coarse grid cell, a 5-minute time slot, the gear category, a confidence level, and a random session ID that rotates and resets when you turn sharing off. No precise location, device identifiers, names or account data are sent.

  • The server keeps a hash of your IP address, salted with a value that changes every hour, only to enforce rate limits. The raw IP address is not stored.
  • Reports expire after about 2 hours. Public map reads are delayed by 12–17 minutes.
  • Data is hosted on Vercel and Upstash Redis.

City chat (optional)

  • City chat is off until you open it and agree to the chat rules. If you never open it, nothing in this section applies to you.
  • Opening chat creates an anonymous account with a random ID. No email, phone number, name or password is collected. Your display name is a random handle generated by the server that changes every day.
  • Your chat room is the nearest city, worked out on your phone from the coarse ~150–250 m map area using an offline list of towns. Only the room name (for example “US-IL-champaign”) is sent. Your location, the map cell, device identifiers and scan data are never sent with chat.
  • We store the messages you post (text, time, city room, daily handle and your anonymous ID), your acceptance of the chat rules, reports you make, and the users you block. This is needed to show the feed, enforce the 1-message-per-10-seconds limit and daily caps, and act on reports.
  • Messages are deleted automatically after 24 hours. Messages that were reported are kept for up to 7 days so a moderator can review them, then deleted. You can delete your own messages at any time with the Delete button next to them.
  • Messages are public to anyone in the same city room. Do not post personal information. Automatic filters block phone numbers, street addresses, license plates, email addresses and threats, and moderators may remove posts and ban accounts that break the rules.
  • Chat is hosted on Supabase (supabase.com) and sent over HTTPS. Supabase processes the data on our behalf and, like any web service, sees your IP address when you connect.
  • We may share chat information with authorities when the law requires it or when someone’s safety is at risk. Because chat is anonymous, this is limited to the message, its time, the city room and the random account ID.

Patrol Radar Pro and Google Play subscriptions (Android only)

If you open the Pro purchase screen, the app uses Google Play Billing for checkout and subscription restoration. Google Play, not Patrol Radar, collects payment card and billing details. The recurring U.S. monthly price is $1.99; other regions use the exact price displayed by Google Play. When Google Play offers a free trial on your account, the monthly plan includes a 2-week trial and you are not charged until it ends.

  • For purchase verification, the app creates or reuses a pseudonymous Supabase user ID. The app sends the Google Play purchase token over HTTPS to a secured Supabase Edge Function. Our server asks Google Play for subscription status and expiry and stores the purchase token, a hash, your pseudonymous ID, the verification time and entitlement status. These records are never publicly readable.
  • Google Play sends subscription renewal, expiration, cancellation, hold and refund notices via Google Cloud Pub/Sub, which our server verifies with the Google Play Developer API. A cancellation normally retains Pro through the paid period; a refund/revocation can remove it sooner.
  • To restore a subscription or founding-tester access after you reinstall on the same phone, the Android app derives a one-way hash from an identifier Android assigns to Patrol Radar on your device (never the raw identifier). It is attached to Google Play checkout and sent to our server with verification requests. It cannot be used to identify you in other apps.
  • On a Google Play install of a non-debug build, an install from before November 16, 2026, 7:54 PM Central can unlock Pro on that device. Debug builds and sideloaded builds do not. The app may send the install time and the hash above to our server. A permanent founding grant is recorded only if that server program is turned on, or if you redeem a single-use code. The server stores only a hash of the code. Founding grants do not expire because of subscription cancellation.
  • The app keeps the last verified Pro status on your device so Pro keeps working offline.
  • Subscription status is checked when you open the app or restore purchases. Purchases can be managed or cancelled in Google Play subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Subscription records are retained as needed for fulfillment, fraud prevention, refunds, disputes and legal obligations. To request deletion or unlinking of your pseudonymous account data, contact us below; legal financial recordkeeping may limit immediate removal. Do not include purchase tokens or sensitive payment information in email.

Navigation, Mapbox, and place search (optional)

Navigation Mode uses Mapbox for directions, traffic-aware routing, and guided navigation, and Photon (operated by Komoot, photon.komoot.io) for address and place search. When you search, Photon receives the query and a location bias. When you request a route or start guidance, Mapbox receives the relevant coordinates and may process location during the trip, along with network information such as your IP address. These requests use HTTPS. The app does not attach Bluetooth or Wi-Fi observations to those requests. See the Mapbox Privacy Policy and Komoot's privacy policy.

Navigation is optional: choosing Patrol Mode alone does not send route-search or destination data to Mapbox. If you end the trip, the active Mapbox guidance session stops. App-level recent destinations are not currently retained by Patrol Radar.

Third-party services

  • 2D map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org). That server receives your IP address and the map area being viewed. See the OSMF privacy policy.
  • 3D map tiles and map fonts are loaded from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which serves an OpenMapTiles schema built from OpenStreetMap data. OpenFreeMap receives your IP address and the tiles for the area on screen. See OpenFreeMap.
  • Optional place search uses Photon at photon.komoot.io (Komoot). Optional routing and turn-by-turn guidance use Mapbox. Optional City Chat and Pro verification use Supabase. The optional crowd map uses patrol-radar-api.vercel.app on Vercel, with Upstash Redis when that store is configured. Google Play handles Pro checkout. None of these are used for advertising.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Selling or sharing

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Clear the session in the app or uninstall it to delete everything stored on your device. Shared crowd reports expire automatically in about 2 hours and cannot be linked back to you. Chat messages delete themselves after 24 hours (reported ones within 7 days), and you can delete your own messages sooner. To request removal of a pseudonymous chat or Pro account, contact percolatorbiz@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership and may retain necessary purchase records for refunds, security or legal requirements. Deletion does not cancel Google Play subscriptions.
